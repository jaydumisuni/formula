# D5 — Self-Expansion Architecture

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Status:** FROZEN DESIGN MILESTONE D5  
**Repository name:** temporary only; not product identity  
**Authority:** D1/D1A define constitutional promotion/self-hosting; D2 defines authority/package services; D3 defines discovery compilation; D4 defines executable realizations.

D5 defines how certified discoveries become durable mathematical capability, how future search grammars evolve, how proofs and realizations survive change, and how the machine may improve its own mathematical machinery without circular self-authority.

The central rule is:

> **Self-expansion is generation change under evidence, not mutable self-modification.**

---

## 1. Expansion state

Let:

```text
U_g      = admitted mathematical authority generation
A_g      = activated capability set/closure for U_g
Lambda_g = active discovery/construction grammar under U_g
R_g      = admitted realization set
T_g      = authority-producing toolchain/bootstrap state
```

These identities are related but not collapsed.

A mathematical theorem may change `U` and `A` without changing binaries. A faster compiler may change `R/T` without changing mathematical semantics. A new metaprimitive may change `Lambda/A` while preserving existing theorem truth.

---

## 2. Promotion classes

D5 defines distinct promotion classes because they have different downstream effects.

```text
THEOREM / JUDGEMENT
STRUCTURE WITNESS
COUNTEREXAMPLE / NOGOOD
INVARIANT / CERTIFIED BOUND
REPRESENTATION
REDUCTION
MORPHISM / THEORY INTERPRETATION
DECOMPOSITION / SUFFICIENT SUMMARY
SEMANTIC PRIMITIVE / NEW MATHEMATICAL ENTITY
CAPABILITY
METAPRIMITIVE / SEARCH METHOD
REALIZATION
PACKAGE / THEORY EXTENSION
TOOLCHAIN / CHECKER REALIZATION
```

The promotion policy is class-specific. A true theorem does not automatically become an active rewrite rule; a correct implementation does not automatically become a semantic primitive.

---

## 3. Candidate freeze before certification

A promotable candidate is first structurally frozen:

```text
PromotionCandidate {
    candidate class
    exact semantic artifact digests
    World/foundation
    dependencies
    discovery provenance
    proposed Judgements
    proposed capability effects
    proposed grammar effects
    evidence obligations
}
```

Certification runs against this frozen identity. Search cannot modify the candidate after seeing checker results without creating a new candidate digest.

This prevents proof/certificate evidence from drifting away from the object it supposedly establishes.

---

## 4. Semantic admission vs activation

D5 preserves four distinct states:

```text
CERTIFIED
    evidence establishes a claim

ADMITTED
    claim/entity is part of U_(g+1)

ACTIVATED
    compiler/search may use it automatically

REALIZED
    one or more admitted executable realizations exist
```

The separation is essential.

Examples:

- a theorem may be ADMITTED but not oriented as a rewrite;
- a new reduction may be ADMITTED but remain inactive until interference/cost/reconstruction contracts are checked;
- a new primitive may be ADMITTED with a generic evaluator before a native Realization exists;
- a new metaprimitive may remain in shadow mode even after soundness is proved until transfer/adversarial gates pass.

---

## 5. Primitive formation pipeline

A semantic primitive is not created merely because a construction recurs often.

Canonical formation pipeline:

```text
1. observe repeated certified structure or discover a new characterized object
2. form candidate abstraction/semantic characterization
3. define exact domain/parent/World semantics
4. define selector/normalization/branch/singularity semantics where required
5. prove existence/uniqueness or explicitly define multi-valued semantics
6. derive key laws/structure witnesses
7. falsify over adversarial/negative cases
8. certify semantic Judgements
9. assess compression/reuse/dispatch value
10. run package/composition interference checks
11. admit semantic primitive
12. optionally activate capability
13. compile/validate Realizations under D4
```

A 200-node relational characterization is acceptable. Human closed form/name is optional metadata.

---

## 6. Semantic selector requirement

A new primitive must identify the intended object strongly enough for its scope.

For special-function-like objects this may require:

```text
operator/equation
parent/domain
initial/boundary conditions
normalization
branch convention
analytic continuation/singularity behavior
asymptotic selector
uniqueness theorem
```

For relational/multi-valued objects, uniqueness may intentionally not hold; the result set/selection semantics must then be explicit.

An underdetermined differential equation is not a primitive identity merely because one implementation returns one solution.

---

## 7. Generalization and abstraction formation

D5 recognizes two distinct mathematical growth routes.

### Abstraction formation

Repeated certified constructions are compressed into a more general construction:

```text
C1, C2, ..., Cn -> G(theta)
```

Admission requires theory-bounded generalization, counterexample search, semantic characterization, and proof over the claimed parameter/domain class.

### Theory formation

Existing admitted mathematics yields a genuinely new theorem, relation, concept, reduction, or object not merely a compression of prior syntax.

Both routes return through the same Certification + Promotion boundary.

---

## 8. Reduction and morphism promotion

Reductions/morphisms receive high potential activation value because one edge can unlock large bodies of existing mathematics.

A promoted reduction must declare:

```text
source/target theory
encode relation
decode/reconstruction relation
preserved result classes
assumptions
complexity/resource effects
certificate route
```

A promoted theory morphism/interpretation must bind how source symbols/axioms map into the target and what theorem transport is valid.

Activation may make entire target package capabilities reachable from source problems without duplicating theorems/algorithms.

---

## 9. Structure-witness promotion

A single new structure witness can expand capability closure dramatically.

Example:

```text
before:
    CommutativeRing(D)

after certifying:
    Field(D)

consequence:
    all activated generic Field capabilities become admissible on D
```

D5 therefore treats structure/property proofs as first-class capability-expansion events.

Capability closure is recomputed for `U_(g+1)`; it is never manually edited.

---

## 10. Counterexample/nogood promotion

Failure can expand capability by preventing future wasted search.

Promotable failure artifacts include:

```text
counterexample
minimal conflict support
interpolant
mathematical nogood
impossibility theorem
failed applicability theorem
```

Promotion scope must be exact. A counterexample to one candidate cannot become a universal nogood without a proof of the generalized restriction.

Activated nogoods may prune future CandidateSpaces automatically only within certified theory/World applicability.

---

## 11. Grammar evolution

The active mathematical/discovery grammar is generation-bound:

```text
Lambda_g = admitted/activated constructors + metaprimitives + theory rules
```

When primitive `P` is activated:

```text
Lambda_(g+1) = Lambda_g union {P}
```

CandidateSpace identities therefore bind the grammar/package generation used to construct them.

A candidate space created under `Lambda_g` is not silently reinterpreted under `Lambda_(g+1)`.

The compiler may migrate/recompile it explicitly if doing so is semantically valid and valuable.

---

## 12. Search-language compression

Promotion may shorten future mathematical descriptions dramatically.

Before primitive formation:

```text
93-node composition of existing operators
```

After:

```text
P(x)
```

This changes:

- search depth;
- candidate-space branching;
- proof size;
- compiler route cost;
- human/model explanation options;
- realization specialization opportunities.

D5 therefore records compression/reuse metrics as activation metadata, while truth remains independent of utility.

---

## 13. Distillation of successful campaigns

A repeated certified campaign route may become a deterministic capability.

Example:

```text
R representation
 -> T reduction
 -> I invariant
 -> S solver
```

If applicability and equivalence are established for a problem class, D5 can form candidate primitive:

```text
P = S o I o T o R
```

The resulting primitive is certified as a semantic construction, not merely copied from workflow logs. Generic orchestration/search scaffolding can then be specialized away by D4.

This is the core route from expensive discovery to cheap permanent capability.

---

## 14. Metaprimitive promotion

Metaprimitives alter how future mathematics is discovered, compiled, generalized, or pruned. They receive the strictest activation gate.

Required claims include, where applicable:

```text
soundness theorem
explicit theory/applicability domain
termination/finiteness conditions
completeness scope
preservation/information-loss semantics
interaction surface
resource behavior
fallback/rollback semantics
```

Required empirical/adversarial evidence before automatic activation:

```text
negative controls
adversarial cases
transfer beyond discovery examples
comparison against prior method
shadow-mode campaigns
no authority-policy bypass
```

A metaprimitive may be ADMITTED but remain `SHADOW_ONLY` until activation gates pass.

---

## 15. Shadow activation and staged rollout

Activation modes:

```text
MANUAL_ONLY
SHADOW_ONLY
BOUNDED_AUTOMATIC
DEFAULT_AUTOMATIC
SUPERSEDED
QUARANTINED
```

`SHADOW_ONLY` permits a new capability/metaprimitive to run alongside the active method and produce comparison evidence without influencing the authoritative campaign route.

`BOUNDED_AUTOMATIC` limits domain, budget, or World scope.

This provides safe capability evolution without reducing the mathematical authority standard.

## 16. Package/version semantic change classes

D5 classifies changes by meaning:

```text
REALIZATION_ONLY
DEFINITIONAL_EQUIVALENT
CONSERVATIVE_EXTENSION
THEOREM_STRENGTHENING
ASSUMPTION_WEAKENING
SIGNATURE_CHANGE
NON_CONSERVATIVE_CHANGE

```

This class controls revalidation.

Textual size is irrelevant. A one-line assumption change may invalidate more mathematics than a million-line performance rewrite.

## 17. Generation transition

Canonical transition:

```text
U_g
  + frozen candidates
  + checked Evidence
  + activation/interference policy
  + optional admitted Realizations
      |
      v

      |
      v
```

The transaction records separately:

```text
semantic admission delta
capability activation delta
realization admission delta
toolchain/bootstrap delta
```

These deltas may be bundled in one release but remain semantically distinguishable.

## 18. Capability-closure transition

After `U_(g+1)` commits:

```text
explicit admitted facts
    -> incremental generation-scoped closure
    -> newly applicable capabilities
    -> newly reachable reductions/morphisms
    -> updated compiler indexes
```

Closure generation is deterministic from authority inputs/policy. It cannot invent truth; every derived capability must trace to admitted witnesses/rules.

---

## 19. Proof freshness and dependency cones

Evidence is bound to exact semantic inputs/dependencies.

When a dependency changes, D5 computes the affected authority cone rather than marking the universe globally stale.

Possible states:

```text
UNCHANGED/FRESH
TRANSPORTABLE
REPAIRABLE
RECHECK_REQUIRED
REPROVE_REQUIRED
QUARANTINED
```

Historical evidence remains accessible even when not current for the latest generation.

---

## 20. Proof/certificate transport

If a certified equivalence, theory morphism, conservative extension, or representation equivalence relates old and new semantics, evidence may be transported rather than discarded.

Transport itself is a certified mathematical operation.

Examples:

```text
proof term across type equivalence
lemma across theory interpretation
certificate replay under definitional-equivalent encoding
boundary theorem across conservative package extension
```

The transported Evidence receives a new identity bound to its new target/world/dependencies.

---

## 21. Proof repair

Where transport is insufficient but prior derivation remains mostly valid, D5 allows local proof/certificate repair.

```text
old proof graph
 + semantic diff
 -> affected slice
 -> repair obligations
 -> new checked evidence
```

Repair never means editing a certificate and assuming correctness. The repaired result is independently checked against the new target.

This applies to incremental SAT/proof sequences, program-verification summaries, derived lemmas, and future domain-native certificate families that support repair.

---

## 22. Realization evolution

A semantic primitive may gain faster Realizations without a new mathematical theorem.

```text
R1 generic exact
R2 specialized CPU
R3 SIMD
R4 GPU
R5 incremental
```

Each Realization has independent D4 evidence. Runtime selection may prefer newer/faster versions while old validated realizations remain available for rollback/replay.

A realization-only update must not trigger semantic proof invalidation unless its execution contract changes.

## 23. Self-hosted compiler/checker/metaprimitive evolution

D1A applies fully.

The machine may synthesize/build:

- compiler passes;
- certificate checkers;
- optimizer rules;
- search methods;
- CandidateSpace backends;
- runtime kernels;
- capability inference rules.

But successor authority requires an external-to-successor validation path:

```text
current system generates candidate successor
       |
       v
independent/foundational/bootstrap validation
       |
       v
Promotion
       |
       v
successor admitted in next generation
```

No executable may become trusted because it says it is trustworthy.

## 24. Bootstrap/toolchain generation

D5 tracks separately:

```text
mathematical generation U_g

```

A self-hosted release manifest may bind all five, but rollback/revalidation can reason about them independently.

## 25. Rollback and supersession

Authority history is append-only. Rollback changes the active selected generation; it does not delete later evidence.

A superseded artifact remains addressable with status/lineage:

```text
SUPERSEDED_BY
REFUTED_BY
REPLACED_REALIZATION_BY
WITHDRAWN_FROM_DEFAULT_ACTIVATION
```

If a newly activated metaprimitive causes harmful search behavior without mathematical unsoundness, activation can roll back while its proven theorem remains admitted.

---

## 26. Conflict response

If newly checked evidence conflicts under the same classical World/semantics:

```text
1. quarantine affected admission/activation
2. halt dependent automatic closure
3. identify checker/foundation/dependency conflict cone
4. independently replay evidence paths
5. resolve by invalidating/repairing evidence or explicitly changing foundation/world
6. publish a new generation; never rewrite the historical one
```

Search may investigate the contradiction, but it cannot choose the preferred truth by heuristic score.

---

## 27. Expansion value vs truth

D5 may rank promotable artifacts by utility:

```text
problem-instance reuse
candidate-space compression
capability unlock count
proof/search cost reduction
cross-theory reach
execution speedup
novelty/generalization
```

These influence activation priority and implementation work.

They never determine whether the mathematics is true.

A mathematically true but low-value theorem may remain admitted and inactive.

## 31. D5 frozen laws

1. **Self-expansion is certified generation transition, not mutable self-modification.**
2. **Truth admission, capability activation, and realization admission are separate.**
3. **A primitive's semantics must uniquely/explicitly identify the intended mathematical object or result relation.**
4. **New structure witnesses, reductions, morphisms, invariants, and nogoods can expand capability as meaningfully as new algorithms.**
5. **The discovery grammar evolves only through activated admitted mathematics.**
6. **Metaprimitives receive stricter activation gates because they alter future search behavior.**
7. **Proofs may be reused, transported, or repaired only through certified relations and rechecking.**
8. **Semantic change class, not textual diff size, controls revalidation.**
9. **Self-hosting may generate successors; independent validation must authorize them.**
10. **Historical generations are immutable and rollback-safe.**
11. **Capability growth is proven by future reuse under a stronger generation, not by claiming that discovery occurred.**

D5 is complete when First Light proves one blind discovery, certification, promotion, native realization, and second-query reuse cycle end-to-end.
